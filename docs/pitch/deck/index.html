02 · The problem

Maps plan around time. Nobody plans around the sun.

  • 01In hot cities, the route you pick decides how much direct sun you take, and in summer that is a health risk.
  • 02In dark winters, people get too little daylight. Oslo has about 6 hours of daylight in late December.
  • 03The sun and shade move every minute, so a static “shady street” or “sunny street” list is never right for long.

04 · The product

One engine, two modes.

Shade mode · for hot cities

Finds the coolest route: the most shade and the least direct sun, using air-conditioned links such as the Dubai Metro to Dubai Mall walkway.

Sun mode · for daylight and well-being

Finds the sunniest route for people who want more daylight: winter cities, office workers, anyone short on sun.

Same engine. The preference flips.

09 · Well-being

Daylight and heat, made visible.

UV index

Today’s index with its WHO band, from low to extreme, and the matching sun-protection advice.

Heat stress

A band from apparent temperature, labelled indicative.

Daylight window

Open when the sun is high (your shadow is shorter than you), UV is 3 or more and cloud is under 70%.

Break prompts

Hydration and shade-break reminders on long, hot routes.

Not medical advice

Well-being and daylight guidance only. It does not diagnose or treat anything. Clinicians are future partners, not a claim we make today.

Oslo, in winter

The sun is about 6° high and UV is near zero (0.1). Sun mode there is about daylight and mood, not vitamin D.

10 · The agent

An agent, not just a map.

  • PLANSUnderstands “I’m walking to the metro, I tire easily in the heat” and compares options.
  • CHECKSA second AI step verifies every claim; every number comes from the engine, never made up.
  • ADAPTSIf you are delayed or a street is closed, it replans and asks before changing your plan.

A pluggable provider chain (OpenCode Go, then Voke) with automatic fallback. Until a live key is set, sessions are labelled recorded replays.

How accurate, how fresh.

InputRefresh
Sun positionExact for any minute; within 0.5° of a reference solar model on 20 test samples
ShadowsEvery 10 minutes, on a 2 m grid
Route timingDeparture checked every 5 minutes
WeatherHourly: UV, cloud, sunlight, temperature
Map dataRe-baked from OpenStreetMap on demand; a weekly job is planned, not yet running
Tested

Shadow length and direction for boxes at several sun angles, including Oslo’s 6.6° winter sun. 470 automated tests pass (375 backend, 95 frontend).

Limits, said plainly

About 79% of Dubai buildings have no mapped height, so typical heights are used. Trees and awnings are only what is mapped. A passing cloud is not predicted. No field validation yet, so we claim no accuracy percentage.

Source: docs/ACCURACY.md, docs/VALIDATION.md, docs/PROGRESS.md. Next step: walk the hero routes with a light sensor and compare.

12 · Security and privacy

Nothing to steal: we store no user data.

By design
  • No accounts, no user database
  • Location used per request, then discarded
  • Logs scrub coordinates and keys
  • No ads, no third-party trackers
On the live site
  • Per-IP rate limits, tightest on the AI agent
  • 64 KB request cap, 1,000-character text cap
  • CORS locked to our domain
  • CSP, HSTS, no framing (except the hotel widget)
Secrets and audit
  • LLM key stays on the server
  • pip-audit: no known vulnerabilities
  • npm audit: 2 findings, both in Next’s build-time PostCSS; the fix is a major upgrade

We aim to align with the UAE PDPL and the GDPR by design. This is an aim, not a certification. No external penetration test yet; it comes before any B2B launch.

Source: docs/SECURITY.md. Controls implemented in backend/zill/api/security.py and frontend/next.config.ts (2026-10-23).

14 · Business model

Freemium. No ads.

No ads, because selling attention would mean selling location.

Keeping location out of any ad pipeline keeps the privacy story clean.

  • 01Free app for individuals: reach and trust.
  • 02Optional premium: saved routes and day plans.
  • 03B2B and B2G subscriptions (fleets, hotels, employers, cities) as the main revenue.
  • 04A pay-per-route API for delivery and mobility apps.
